          How it works

          Weather moves. So do you.
          We line the two up.

          1. 01

            Route it

            Pick a start, a destination and when you're leaving. We find the fastest driving route and pin a checkpoint every few dozen miles.

          2. 02

            Time it

            For every checkpoint we work out when you'll actually get there — then pull that place's forecast for that exact hour.

          3. 03

            Score it

            Rain, snow, ice, fog and gusts roll up into a single road score — for now, and for every hour you could leave over the next three days.

          Features

          Built for the drive,
          not the destination.

          Arrival-time forecasts

          Not "today in Sacramento" — the sky over Sacramento at 10:42 AM, when you're there.

          Best time to leave

          A 72-hour scan shows whether waiting two hours dodges the storm. Tap any hour to re-plan instantly.

          Ice & snow alerts

          Freezing temps plus moisture get flagged loudly — the stuff that turns a pass into a parking lot.

          Pack-list nudges

          A 25° swing between start and finish? You'll know to throw a jacket in the back seat.

          Share a link

          Every plan has a link. Send it to whoever's riding shotgun.

          No keys, no sign-up

          Open data from Open-Meteo, OSRM and OpenStreetMap. Works on your phone at the gas station.

          The story

          Made for explorers,
          by explorers.

          Weather to Travel started as a bootcamp project by four friends who kept getting caught in the rain halfway to somewhere sunny. This is the rebuild: same idea, sharper tools.
